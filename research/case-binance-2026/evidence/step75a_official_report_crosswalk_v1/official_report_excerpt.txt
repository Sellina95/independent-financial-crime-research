VERIFIED OFFICIAL STATEMENTS:

1. Between 20:05 UTC and 20:09 UTC,
   five Binance hot wallets sent 233.46 million XRP
   to an attacker-controlled address.

2. Binance hot wallets on Solana and Optimism
   sent approximately 970,500 SOL and 44.2 million OP.

3. A Binance hot wallet sent 18,270 BTC
   to an attacker-controlled address.

4. The attacker exploited Ethereum's EIP-7702
   authorization mechanism.

5. The final unauthorized transfer of 2,961.9 ETH
   finalized on-chain at 23:56 UTC.

6. At 00:10 UTC on 30 October,
   Binance executed a new authorization from Binance14
   to the zero address, clearing the delegation code.
