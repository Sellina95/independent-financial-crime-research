# AXIOM — Gate 2 Incident Attribution Review V1

## Objective
Distinguish PoR association, observed transfers,
and evidence of unauthorized incident outflows.

## Verified evidence inventory
- 11/11 candidate addresses match the supplied PoR CSV.
- 98 PoR asset-level rows, not 98 independent wallets.
- Bitcoin: 11 native transactions audited.
- TRON: 2 native transactions audited.
- XRPL: 5 native payment transactions audited.
- Ethereum: transaction/event evidence exists but
  address-level initial-loss attribution remains unresolved.
- Solana: no direct first-hop transfer corroborated.

## Critical distinction
- PoR listing supports a historical address association.
- Native transfer verification proves movement, not theft.
- Temporal proximity does not prove unauthorized conduct.
- Internal relocation has not been excluded.
- The five XRPL accounts are distinct source accounts.
- BTC/TRX/XRP quantities are not USD loss estimates.
- Do not add downstream transfers to initial losses.

## Missing decisive evidence
1. Authenticate pre-incident PoR publication provenance.
2. Identify independent incident-specific TX attribution.
3. Test internal transfer / authorized sweep alternatives.
4. Distinguish victim source from intermediary receiver.

## Decision
- Gate 2: OPEN.
- Independently confirmed unauthorized victim wallets: 0.
- Evidence matrix is an investigation aid, not a verdict.
