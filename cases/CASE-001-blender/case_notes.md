## 6. Temporal Boundary

The observed transactions occurred before the May 6, 2022 OFAC action.

This research is retrospective reconstruction, not a demonstration that the transactions could have been identified as illicit in real time.

## 7. Next Verification Step

Inspect the full input and output structure of Transaction B, including output amounts, transaction fee, and subsequent spending activity.

No conclusions about recipient identity or criminal intent will be drawn from transaction connectivity alone.
